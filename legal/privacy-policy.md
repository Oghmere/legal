## 2. Information we collect

We collect personal information that you provide, that is generated as you use the Service, and that we receive from service providers acting on our behalf.

**a. Account and profile information.** Name, email address, password (stored in hashed form), and account settings when you register.

**b. Your Content.** The manuscripts, notes, prompts, files, project data, and other material you create, upload, or generate through the Service. Your Content may itself contain personal information that you choose to include.

**c. Payment information.** When you purchase Credits or subscriptions, our payment processor (Stripe) collects and processes your payment details. We receive limited billing information (such as transaction records and the last digits/card type) but **do not store full payment-card numbers**.

**d. Usage and device information.** Log data such as IP address, browser and device type, pages and features accessed, timestamps, referring URLs, and diagnostic and performance data. This is used to operate, secure, and improve the Service.

**e. Communications.** Information you provide when you contact us for support, submit feedback, or otherwise communicate with us.

**f. Cookies and similar technologies.** We use strictly necessary and functional cookies/local storage to keep you signed in and remember preferences. See Section 9.

**g. Writing-activity information (optional, opt-in).** If — and only if — you turn on writing-activity tracking, we collect **behavioural information about how you write** so we can show you a private progress dashboard, insights, timeline, and achievements. This is a separate, express-consent choice that is **off by default**; capture does nothing until you enable it in your settings, and we record the version of this Policy you consented to.

   - **What we collect:** writing- and revision-session timing (when a session started and ended and its duration), the number of words added or removed in a session, which project or document a session belonged to, your local time-zone offset, per-day aggregates (words written, minutes written, session and revision-session counts, documents touched, stories completed), streak and cadence figures derived from those aggregates, structural counts about a document (such as word and sentence totals), and the achievement badges you unlock.
   - **What we do *not* collect:** we **never store the text you wrote.** No manuscript content, sentences, or characters of Your Content are written to this data — only the numeric and timing measurements above.
   - **Why we collect it:** solely to compute and display **your own** progress and writing habits back to you. It is **not** used for advertising, is **not** sold, is **not** used to train AI models, and is **not** shared with other users.
   - **Your control:** it stays private to your account and is protected by row-level access controls so that only you can see it. You can turn it off at any time in your settings; when you do, we stop collecting and can delete the writing-activity data already collected. Deleting your account also deletes this data (see Section 7).

## 3. How we use your information and our legal basis

Under PIPA, we collect, use, and disclose personal information for purposes a reasonable person would consider appropriate in the circumstances, and with your consent (express or implied) except where the law permits or requires otherwise. We use personal information to:

   (a) provide, operate, maintain, and secure the Service and your account;
   (b) process Your Content through AI models and other service providers to generate the features you request (see Section 4);
   (c) publish, share, or distribute Your Content **only at your direction** (for example, when you create a public share page or export Your Content);
   (d) process payments and manage Credits and subscriptions;
   (e) provide support, respond to your requests, and communicate service-related notices;
   (f) monitor, troubleshoot, prevent abuse and fraud, and enforce our Terms;
   (g) understand usage and improve and develop the Service;
   (h) where you have turned it on, compute and show you your own private writing-activity dashboard, insights, timeline, and achievements (Section 2g); and
   (i) comply with legal obligations and protect our rights and the rights and safety of others.

We rely primarily on your consent, on the necessity of processing to provide a service you have requested, and on our legitimate operational and security needs, as permitted by PIPA. **We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use Your Content to train any AI model — ours or anyone else's.**

## 4. AI processing of Your Content

Certain features send Your Content (such as manuscripts, notes, and prompts) to **third-party AI providers** so they can generate coaching, feedback, or other AI output at your request. This means:

   - Your Content is transmitted to and processed by AI providers acting as our service providers; and
   - those providers may store and process Your Content **outside Canada, including in the United States** (see Section 6).

We select AI providers that offer terms limiting their use of submitted content to providing the service (for example, providers that do not use API-submitted content to train their models). We list our current AI providers in Section 5.

### If you use the Community Showcase (public content)

The **Community Showcase** lets you publish work to a shared, public space. This is always your choice — nothing you create is public unless you publish it. If you do publish:

   - the work you publish and the **author profile** you create (your handle, display name, bio, and any links) become **public** — visible to other users and, for public pages, to anyone with the link;
   - other users can leave feedback, reviews, and votes, which are associated with their accounts;
   - reports you file and feedback you leave are associated with your account and used for moderation and abuse prevention; and
   - public content may be seen, copied, and shared by others. Unpublishing or deleting stops further distribution by us but may not undo copies others have already made.

## 7. Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, to provide the Service, and to meet legal, accounting, and security requirements. When you delete Your Content or your account, we will delete or de-identify the associated personal information within a reasonable period, except that residual copies may persist in encrypted backups for a limited time and information may be retained where required by law or to resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. If you turn off writing-activity tracking (Section 2g), we stop collecting it and can delete the writing-activity data already gathered for you.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against loss, theft, and unauthorized access, use, or disclosure — including encryption in transit, access controls, and hashed credentials. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach that creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected individuals and applicable authorities as required by law.

## 9. Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar local-storage technologies that are strictly necessary to operate the Service (such as keeping you signed in) and functional cookies that remember your preferences. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling necessary cookies may affect how the Service works. We use operational logging (Section 2d) for diagnostics and security rather than advertising, and **we do not use your information for third-party advertising**.
