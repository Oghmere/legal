# Your rights in 60 seconds

*A plain-language summary to show at signup. It's a friendly overview — the full [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md), [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md), and [Acceptable Use & AI Policy](./acceptable-use-policy.md) are what actually govern your account. If anything here differs from those, they win.*

---

### ✍️ Your work stays yours
You own everything you write and everything the AI helps you create. We don't claim any of it. To run Oghmere, you give us just enough permission to store your work, run it through AI when you ask, and publish or share it **only when you tell us to** — nothing more.

### 🚫 We won't sell it or train on it
We don't sell your content, and we never use it to train any AI model — ours or anyone else's.

### 🔗 You decide what's public
Sharing only happens when you choose it. You control who sees what, and you can turn sharing off anytime.

### 🤖 About the AI
When you use AI features, your content is sent to trusted AI providers (like Anthropic and Google) to do the work — this may happen in the U.S. AI can be wrong, so give its output a human review before you rely on it. It's a writing coach, not professional advice.

### 🔒 Your privacy
We collect your account info, the content you create, payment details (through Stripe), and basic usage data — to run the Service, support you, and keep it secure. You can access, correct, or delete your information, or withdraw consent, anytime by contacting our Privacy Officer. We follow BC's privacy law (PIPA) and Canada's PIPEDA.

### 📈 Progress tracking is optional
Want to see your writing streaks and stats? You can turn on writing-activity tracking to power a private progress dashboard. It's **off unless you switch it on**, we only measure *how* you write (times, word counts, streaks) — never *what* you wrote — and you can turn it off and delete the data whenever you like.

### 💳 Credits & payments
Some AI features use Credits. There's a free tier to start. Paid Credits go through Stripe and are non-refundable, except where BC or Canadian consumer law says otherwise.

### 🧭 Play fair
Don't upload illegal or infringing content, don't misuse the AI, and don't game the Credit system. Full details are in the Acceptable Use & AI Policy.

### 👤 Age
You must be 13 or older. If you're under the age of majority where you live (19 in BC), you need a parent or guardian's OK.

### 🍁 Where we stand
Oghmere is run by a British Columbia company. These agreements are governed by BC and Canadian law.
